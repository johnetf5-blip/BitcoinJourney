What Is Bitcoin Self-Custody?

Self-custody means taking responsibility for controlling access to your own Bitcoin, instead of relying on an exchange or another company to hold it for you.

1. What is a Bitcoin wallet?

A Bitcoin wallet is software or a physical device that helps you manage your Bitcoin keys and create transactions.

Your Bitcoin is not physically stored inside the wallet. The Bitcoin exists on the Bitcoin network. Your wallet helps you prove control over the coins associated with your keys.

2. Public keys and private keys

Think of a public address like an account number that you can share to receive Bitcoin.

A private key is secret information that allows you to authorise spending Bitcoin.

  • Public address: Share it when receiving Bitcoin.
  • Private key: Keep it secret and secure.

Anyone who obtains the necessary private keys may be able to spend the associated Bitcoin.

3. What is a recovery phrase?

Many wallets use a recovery phrase, often made up of 12 or 24 words. It can help restore access to a wallet if the original device is lost or damaged.

The words represent sensitive backup information from which wallet keys can be derived.

Important security rule

Never share your real recovery phrase with anyone. Never type it into a website, send it in a message, or upload a photograph of it.

Anyone who obtains it may be able to take your Bitcoin.

4. Exchange custody versus self-custody

When Bitcoin is held on an exchange, the company generally controls the private keys. You rely on that company to give you access and process withdrawals.

With self-custody, you control the private keys yourself. This gives you more direct control, but also more responsibility.

  • Exchange custody: you depend on the provider.
  • Self-custody: you protect your own keys and backups.

5. How can you protect your Bitcoin?

  • Keep your recovery phrase private.
  • Make a secure offline backup.
  • Protect your wallet device with a strong PIN.
  • Verify wallet software and device authenticity.
  • Be careful of fake support messages and phishing websites.
  • Test your backup recovery process safely before relying on it.

Remember: self-custody gives you control, but losing your backup or exposing your keys can mean losing access to Bitcoin.
