About this project

Knowledge before decisions.

Bitcoin Journey is an independent educational project designed to make Bitcoin concepts and calculations easier to understand. The tools provide estimates, not predictions.

This website is educational and does not provide financial, investment or tax advice. Always check calculations and consider fees and risks before making financial decisions.
